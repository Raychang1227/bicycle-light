SMART BICYCLE TAIL LIGHT SYSTEM
Short English introduction, with Traditional Chinese reference

ENGLISH

Hello, this is my Smart Bicycle Tail Light System.

It helps cyclists show turns, braking, and emergencies more clearly.

The front controller connects wirelessly to the rear light through Bluetooth.

I press A to signal left, and B to signal right. Pressing both buttons activates the hazard lights.

When the system detects braking, the red lights flash and the buzzer sounds.

In demonstration mode, tilting the model can trigger an SOS light and sound warning.

I can also adjust settings and record test data using an offline webpage on my phone.

This prototype still needs further testing. Thank you!

繁體中文對照

大家好，這是我的智慧自行車尾燈系統。

它能幫助騎士更清楚地表達轉彎、煞車和緊急狀況。

車頭控制器透過藍牙，無線連接車尾燈。

按 A 表示左轉，按 B 表示右轉，同時按下兩個按鈕，可以開啟雙黃警示燈。

系統偵測到煞車時，紅燈會閃爍，蜂鳴器也會發出提示音。

在展示模式下，傾斜模型可以觸發 SOS 聲光警示。

我也能使用手機的離線網頁，調整設定並記錄測試資料。

這套原型仍需要進一步測試，謝謝大家！

Demonstration reminder: tilt must exceed the configured angle for the configured confirmation period. Clear SOS with a continuous three-second A+B hold.
